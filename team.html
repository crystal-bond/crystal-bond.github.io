Crystal BOND / Team

Consortium and people

Physicists, engineers and technicians of four INFN units, with the universities of Ferrara, Padova and Trieste.

National coordinatorAndrea MazzolariINFN and University of Ferrara · andrea.mazzolari@unife.it
FerraraINFN Ferrara

Coordination, crystal shaping, bonding and metrology

The Ferrara group makes crystals for channeling, X-ray and γ-ray optics in a 130 m² cleanroom; its bent silicon crystals collimate the LHC beams. It buys and runs the bonding equipment, prepares the crystals and characterises the bent assemblies.

Local coordinator
Andrea Mazzolari
With
Vincenzo Guidi, Piero Rosati, Francesco Mollica, Lorenzo Malagutti, Marco Romagnoni, Melissa Tamisari, Lisa Ferro, Arianna Rossi, Muhammad Hamza
LegnaroINFN Laboratori Nazionali di Legnaro

FEM design, thin films, interface analysis

Semiconductor microfabrication, especially germanium, with the Physics and Astronomy Department of the University of Padova. Sputtering and e-beam deposition of the films for thermo-compression bonding, Rutherford backscattering, SEM and a new ion mill for cross-sections of the bonded interface; finite-element models of bonded crystals.

Local coordinator
Davide De Salvador
With
Enrico Napolitani, Francesco Sgarbossa, Enrico Di Russo, Stefano Bertoldo, Davide Valzani, Sara Carturan, Gianluigi Maggioni
TriesteINFN Trieste

Synchrotron tests and spectral imaging

Phase-contrast and spectral imaging with synchrotron light since 1997, mainly at the SYRMEP beamline of Elettra and the INFN PEPI lab. It designs the Laue crystals for imaging, the alignment mechanics and the phantoms, and runs the beam tests.

Local coordinator
Fulvia Arfelli
With
Ralf Hendrik Menk, Luigi Rigon, Luca Brombal, Luca Sbuelz
FrascatiINFN Laboratori Nazionali di Frascati

Crystal design, X-ray optics and tomography

Channeling of beams and radiation and X-ray optics at the XlabF laboratory, with its polycapillary optics unit. It leads the design of crystals and substrates and characterises crystals by X-ray computed tomography.

Local coordinator
Sultan Dabagov
With
Dariush Hampai, Augusto Marcelli, Zeinab Ebrahimpour, Alessandro Curcio, Florin Sirghi, Alessandro Scordo

Supporting institutions

Laboratories, universities and a company that wrote in support of the project and are interested in its crystals.

  • CERN · Geneva

    The TWOCRYST study at the LHC: bent crystals that deflect TeV beams for fixed-target and dipole-moment experiments.

  • ESRF · Grenoble

    The crystal and crystal-analyser laboratories, makers of bonded silicon analysers, on new materials for analysers.

  • European XFEL · Schenefeld

    Optics for imaging and beam steering at X-ray free-electron lasers.

  • Elettra Sincrotrone Trieste · Trieste

    The optics group; synchrotron tests at the SYRMEP beamline.

  • INAF · Italy

    Hard X-ray and γ-ray astronomy: Laue lenses for future missions.

  • Lund University

    Lund University · Lund

    Efficient focusing optics for low-energy X-rays, for faster X-ray imaging.

  • University of Saskatchewan

    University of Saskatchewan · Saskatoon

    Bent Laue crystals for spectral and phase-contrast imaging at the Canadian Light Source.

  • University of Trieste

    University of Trieste · Trieste

    Astroparticle physics and γ-ray astronomy.

  • CINEL Strumenti Scientifici

    CINEL Strumenti Scientifici · Vigonza (Padova)

    Synchrotron instrumentation: a route from prototypes to beamlines worldwide.
